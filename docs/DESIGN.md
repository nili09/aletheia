# Aletheia design: Jantar Mantar at midnight

Dark-first, typography-led, minimal decoration. The sky is the interface; the numbers are the heroes.

## Colour (no gradients, no colourful palettes)

* Ground #010101. Text #ffffff.
* Sandstone #bdad96 for instrument geometry: rings, arcs, nakshatra divisions, gnomons.
* Marble #f1f0ea for scales, ticks and fine markings.
* Accent #dff140 only for the current selection, as a fill at about 33% opacity. Never decorative.
* Grahas are points of white light sized by their true apparent brightness. The Moon shows its real phase. Rahu and Ketu, being mathematical points, are hollow sandstone marks.

## Type

* Latin: Helvetica Now Display if licensed for the web; otherwise Inter Tight (free). Negative letter-spacing on Latin text, always.
* Devanagari: Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit for verses, Noto Sans Devanagari for interface text. Letter-spacing 0 on Devanagari, always, so the headline stroke stays joined.
* Tabular figures for every number. Times to the second, longitudes to the arcsecond.
* Self-host every font so the app works offline.

## Motion and controls

* Sky objects move at real speed or a stated multiple of it. Nothing bounces.
* Interface transitions: 0.3 s ease-out.
* Controls are transparent at 60% opacity and go to full opacity when pressed. Corner radius 2 px.

## Two worlds, one family

* Vedhashala is spacious: one idea per screen.
* Prayogashala is denser and quieter: tables, distributions, numbers first.
