# Installing Aletheia on your phone

Aletheia is a web app that installs to your home screen and then works offline, full screen, like any other app. Nothing to download from an app store.

Open the app's address in your phone's browser first: the link Nilesh sent you (it ends in `.workers.dev`).

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open the address in **Safari**. (Other browsers on iOS may not offer the option.)
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow) in the toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Keep the name "Aletheia" and tap **Add**.
5. Open Aletheia from the new icon on your home screen. It opens full screen, without Safari's address bar.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**).
4. Confirm with **Install**.
5. Open Aletheia from the home screen or the app drawer.

## Updates and offline use

* After the first visit the app works without a connection.
* New versions download quietly in the background and take effect the next time you open the app.
* To remove it, long-press the icon and choose **Remove** (iPhone) or **Uninstall** (Android).
